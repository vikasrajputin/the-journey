About

An exhibition built on evidence

THE JOURNEY tells the interconnected story of existence, from the early universe to the world you live in today. It is meant to be read, explored and questioned.

Our approach

  • Evidence comes before spectacle. Pictures are there to explain, not to stand in for explanation.
  • We say what is known, what is estimated, what is debated and what is still unknown.
  • Dates are rounded or given as ranges where the evidence calls for it.
  • Religious, philosophical and cultural traditions are treated with respect. We keep faith claims separate from claims that can be tested independently.
  • Illustrations and reconstructions are always labelled. They are never presented as photographs.

What this is not

This is not a game. There are no scores, badges, quizzes or locked chapters. You can read in any order, skip anything, and come back whenever you like.
