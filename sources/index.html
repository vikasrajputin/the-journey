Every fact in the Chapter 1 game comes from here, level by level. Every source was found on its publisher’s own site; 7 of 8 have been re-read against the exact claim they support.

How sources are chosen and checked

We prefer peer-reviewed research, museums, universities and public scientific agencies. Each entry says what it is used to support, and any caveats, such as a press release or a single study.

Entries marked “claim-by-claim check pending” are on publisher sites that could not be read automatically; a person still needs to confirm them.
