How sources are chosen and checked

We prefer peer-reviewed research, museums, universities and public scientific agencies. Each entry says what it is used to support, and any caveats, such as a press release or a single study.

Entries marked “claim-by-claim check pending” are on publisher sites that could not be read automatically; a person still needs to confirm them. Read more about how evidence is labelled.
